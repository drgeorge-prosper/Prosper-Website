Prosper Continuum

Continuing your progress.

Every Prosper Clarity programme ends with a clear recommendation for what comes next. Prosper Continuum is structured, ongoing support to keep you supported and moving forward.

What it is

Support that continues after the plan.

A single programme gets you a clear plan. Prosper Continuum keeps that plan alive: regular check-ins, early course-correction, and a doctor who already knows your history rather than starting from zero each time.

Quarterly

Structured check-ins every three months.

Six-monthly

Twice-yearly review and recalibration.

Recommended clinically, at your Prosper Clarity review. Never sold upfront, and never a condition of starting your first programme.
